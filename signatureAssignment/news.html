News and Updates

Stay up to date with what is happening at Birds of Prey Lacrosse. From new product launches to sponsorship announcements, this is where you will find it all.

Signature Head Now Available

Spring 2025

After months of development and player testing, our flagship Signature Lacrosse Head is officially available. Designed for attackers and midfielders who want a lightweight, responsive feel, the head is now listed on our products page. Early feedback from beta testers has been outstanding.

First Team Sponsorship Partnership Announced

Winter 2025

Birds of Prey is proud to announce its first official team sponsorship. We will be providing gear and apparel to a regional club lacrosse program this coming season. This partnership reflects our commitment to supporting the lacrosse community at the grassroots level and getting quality equipment into the hands of players who need it.

Game Jersey Added to the Lineup

Fall 2024

Our navy blue and gold Game Jersey is now part of the Birds of Prey product lineup. Made from moisture-wicking fabric and designed for comfort during full-game conditions, the jersey is available in sizes small through extra large. Team orders of ten or more jerseys qualify for a discount. Contact us for details.

Birds of Prey Lacrosse Officially Founded

Summer 2024

Griffin and Zack officially launched Birds of Prey Lacrosse with a simple mission: build gear that players can trust at a price that makes sense. What started as a conversation between two teammates turned into a full brand with a product line, a website, and a growing community. We are just getting started.
